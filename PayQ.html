What is an EPC QR Code (GiroCode)?

Standardized by the European Payments Council (EPC069-12), it allows any European mobile banking app (ING, KBC, Belfius, BNP Paribas, Sparkasse, N26, Revolut, Bunq, Erste, etc.) to immediately pre-fill payment transfers without typing errors.

Why Emojis Are Excluded

The SEPA EPC standard strictly enforces standard Latin characters. Including emojis in payment references causes banking applications to reject the QR code or fail during automated interbank clearing.
