Strict Trail Rule: Autonomie Totale (No Ravito)

No Official Aid Stations or Refreshments along the route!

There will be NO water stations (ravito) or food distributions set up along the trails. The Presqu'île coastal paths can be warm, sunny, and exposed. You are required to be 100% self-sufficient from start to finish.
